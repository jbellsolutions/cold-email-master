# Conflict Resolutions

Before this skill, Justin's cold email rules lived in about 20 skills that disagreed. This table records each conflict, what this skill decided, and why. Decided 2026-10-04. When a future source disagrees, add a row; don't silently change a rule.

| # | Conflict | Who said what | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Length** | Frameworks 50 to 100 (newest docs 85 to 100); trades v2 60 to 100; universal-referral 150 to 165; Buchan skill 120 to 200; outreach-composer and QC agent 120 cap | Framework emails 60 to 100, aim 80 to 95; follow-ups 30 to 50; Closer 1 under 50; Closer 2 under 100 | Hormozi: half a page, about 80 words, and the reading-level evidence. Buchan's 120 to 200 is labelled in his own file as "ours, not Buchan's". The universal-referral skill's longer referral email stays its own declared exception. |
| 2 | **Reading level** | House: 8th grade; Buchan: "Hemingway grade 6" (unverified legacy) | Target grade 5 or lower; ceiling 7 | Hormozi: templates rewritten below 3rd grade raised response 50%. Grade 5 is achievable without sounding childish. |
| 3 | **Dashes** | Frameworks, cold-email-qa, spam-word-checker: none. editorial-workflow: regular dashes. charm-qc-gate: requires em dashes | No em or en dashes anywhere | The em dash is read as an AI tell. charm-qc-gate's rule is rejected. |
| 4 | **Urgency** | charm-qc-gate: "urgency present", direct commands. Frameworks: no fake urgency. Buchan: never fix a draft with urgency | No fake urgency or hard commands; structurally true scarcity only, back half | House and Buchan agree, and urgency is a spam signal. charm-qc-gate's rule is rejected. |
| 5 | **Numbers** | Hormozi: exact numbers, never ranges. House: revenue line "built to add {honest range}" | Exact numbers for real past results. Forward-looking claims stay "built to add" or "the target is", never a precise promise | Both are right about different things: precision sells proof; precision in a promise is a fabrication. |
| 6 | **First-touch ask** | Master template v1: no call ask on first touch. Trades v2 and universal-referral: direct number ask. Hermes agent: never ask for a call | A per-doc setting in the header. Default: walkthrough close. Direct ask allowed when declared | Justin's own newer docs deliberately use the direct ask for referral/trades; the walkthrough remains the general default. |
| 7 | **"Free"** | spam-word-checker bans "free", "no cost", "no strings". House Closer 2 offers "the free thing, no strings". Real Experts: "no charge" once per lead | "Free" never in a subject or touch 1. Prefer "at no charge" or "yours either way". "Free" allowed once, in Closer 2 | Keeps the house gift mechanic while respecting the spam-word evidence where it matters most (subject and first touch). |
| 8 | **Opt-out** | Buchan skill: opt-out on first touch and sequences, postal address for US. Frameworks: silent. spam-word-checker: no "silence = no" | Sequencer unsubscribe on every step and US postal address are pre-launch checks. A plain-voice opt-out line in Closer 1 and Closer 2, optional in follow-ups. Never "silence = no" | Compliance without spending 15 of a framework email's 100 words on boilerplate. |
| 9 | **Greeting** | spam-word-checker: "John," only. Frameworks: "Hey Rosa,". Buchan: "Greetings", "Bonjour". editorial-workflow: all lowercase | `Hey {first_name},` default, locale swaps, spun at word level | Matches every live house doc. Buchan's unusual greetings are humour devices, out per Justin. |
| 10 | **Subjects** | Frameworks: 1 to 4 words, lowercase, vague. personalization-agent: company name. Buchan: intriguing or funny. Eric: about 20 spintax variants | 1 to 4 words, lowercase, vague; 3+ spun variants per framework. Competitor open-loop subject only as a declared test | House rule kept; spintax added for fingerprinting. The competitor subject is the Instantly narrator's untested adaptation. |
| 11 | **Cadence** | House: 22 touches, new thread per framework, 3-day gaps. Buchan: 8 in-thread, 5 working days. Hermes agent: 4 emails days 0/3/7/14. outreach-composer: 4 over 3 weeks. Eric: 4 emails + caller | 22-touch Sept 3 policy is the default. Sprint mode (touch 1 + follow-up + reply cadence + caller) added as a declared option | Justin's Sept 3 decision stands. Eric's data that touch 1 gets about 99% of positives argues for hardest QC on touch 1, not for cutting the sequence. |
| 12 | **Humour** | House: plain, no jokes. Buchan and Charm plugin: humour formulas | None. Buchan techniques only (honesty, Way In, escape hatches, opt-out craft, reply model, follow-up types) | Justin's choice, 2026-10-04. `jon-buchan-copywriter` remains for when he explicitly wants Buchan's voice. |
| 13 | **Placeholders and spintax** | House docs: `{town}`, `{sender}` single braces. Instantly: `{{RANDOM \| a \| b}}` | Single braces = instructions for the writing agent inside a doc, never shipped. Double-brace Instantly spintax = the only braces allowed in upload-ready copy. Verifier renders option 1 before counting | Keeps every existing doc valid while adding spintax. (The plan proposed `{{snake_case}}` for per-lead vars; changed during build because it would break all live docs, and per-lead copy is fully written by the agent anyway.) |
| 14 | **Data vendors in the videos** | Eric and Hormozi/Instantly name Clay, Apollo, Ocean, Prospeo, LeadMagic, Blitz, QuickEnrich, Multisets, GetLeads, Exa, Parallel | Methods kept (signal-paired lists, seed lookalikes, permutation + verify, homepage scoring, 1,000-send verdicts).
