# Line Banks (runtime reference)

Real Buchan lines to riff on, grouped by job. Nothing here is invented; full pieces are in swipe-examples.md. **Tags:** `[verbatim]` = Buchan's own words (incl. copy he wrote for clients, and his `{slot}` templates) · `[student example]` = Aaron Krall's annotated email (kept only where it's the sole instance) · `· OCR` = from a slide image · `· transcript` = spoken in a lesson · `· 3rd-party` = someone else's transcription · `· list` = written to an opted-in list or group, **not** cold outreach. `· interview` = podcast/interview transcript (whisper), `· captions` = YouTube captions; cited `[INT <slug> m:ss]` (`[INT reddit]` and `[INT selzy]` are his own writing, no timestamp). Prospects' own replies are excluded. `[NL <date> <slug>]` = newsletter (knowledge/09c): `· list` unless tagged `(template)`, i.e. his own prospect-facing template copy quoted in the email.

**Using the banks.** One formula once per email: "Don't use it more than once in a particular email or letter" [PDF L09 p43]. "Funny, Serious, Funny, Serious" [L03 32:07]. Adapt the line to the sender and prospect; never paste it in unchanged.

## Drafting checklist
1. **Pick one line per job** (greeting, way in, offer hinge, ask, way out, closer, sign-off, titles), and never two from the same formula in one message.
2. **Adapt, don't paste:** swap names, pets, clients and offers for the sender's real ones; keep the shape and the rhythm.
3. **Prefer `[verbatim]`.** A `[student example]` line must not be presented as Buchan's own.
4. **Respect the channel tag:** `· list` lines and the deadline lines stay out of cold outreach; anti-lines are negative examples only.
5. **Subject lines:** cold ones exploit being a stranger or name the letter's oddity; the "drunk cold email" title is podcast/post only.
6. **Warm replies** take at most one light line (a footnote or the silly third title), not a run of gags.
7. **Every prop is real:** if the sender has no pet photo or letter, don't reference one.
