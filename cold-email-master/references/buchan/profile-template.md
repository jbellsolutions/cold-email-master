# Sender profile template (Buchan, Lesson 1)

Fill in with the sender's real answers and save as `buchan-profile.md`. "You" can mean the person or
their company. **If an answer isn't genuine or you can't defend it, leave it blank.** Never invent
proof, clients or numbers. The source is `knowledge/02-story-and-offer.md`.

```markdown
# Sender Profile — {Name / Company}

## Your Story
- Great at (up to 5 core skills): {…}
- Stand for (3 values or things you want to be known for; e.g. craft / helping people / a value): {…}
- Against (up to 3 issues that exist in your sector): {…}
- Obstacles faced (e.g. a problem with your industry or old job / a struggle that will resonate /
  an advantage you didn't have; personal issues are fine if they affected the business): {…}
- Ambitions (not about money; e.g. self-focused / focused on others / overarching goal): {…}
- Weakness you'll call out honestly and turn into an advantage (optional): {…}
- Story in a few lines (the beats can flex):
  - Identity (who you were / what you did): {…}
  - Struggle: {…}
  - Discovery: {…}
  - Surprise (what unexpectedly worked): {…}
  - What you do now: {…}
- Story title or hook (intriguing, ideally a little oxymoronic): {…}

## Your Ideal Clients
- Who you can help most (job titles): {…}
- Sectors (prioritise ones you're excited about): {…}
- Locations: {…}
- Company size (employees): {…}
- Attributes you're drawn to (used for Liking, e.g. ambitious, funny, disruptive): {…}
- Profile keywords (traits, skills, niche terms, trends): {…}
- Timing triggers (e.g. changed jobs in the past 90 days): {…}
- Bonus: your passion for / understanding of their sector (key terms, trends, jargon): {…}

## Your Offer
- Pain points you solve (general): {…}
- Problems with existing solutions: {…}
- Pain points you help them avoid: {…}
- Tie to time / money / reduction in stress: {…}
- One-liner: "I help {who} {result} without {pain avoided}": {…}
- Fear-quashing mechanism or guarantee (only if real and low-risk for you): {…}
- Proof (at least one; real only): results, case studies, testimonials, recognisable clients
  across sectors, real reply screenshots, coverage or podcasts: {…}
- The ask (sell the call as "not a terrible idea"; e.g. 7-minute chat, ideas free to steal): {…}
- Mission line for "So why the hell am I writing to you?" bullet 4: {…}

## Voice Assets
- LinkedIn tagline (who you help + how): {…}
- Favourite weapon (pet / funny photo for the close): {…}
- Silly profile or cover photo idea: {…}
- Three job titles (serious / descriptive / silly): {…}
- Sign-offs (e.g. "Have an absolutely wonderful day," / "Fingers crossed," / "Stay vigilant,"): {…}
- Jokes or words that feel like you, and any to avoid: {…}
- Links (group, lead magnet, best podcast or press appearance): {…}
```
