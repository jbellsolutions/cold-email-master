# Reply Playbook: from reply to booked call

The Reply Setter owns this. Every frameworks doc carries a "When they reply" section built from it, in that doc's voice. Source tags as in `copy-rules.md`.

---

## 1. The rules of the reply

- **Any reply pauses the sequence.** Stop-on-reply is on, ideally for the whole company domain. [HOUSE]
- **Speed to contact: minutes, not hours.** [HORMOZI] The house setter or reply agent drafts immediately; Justin approves sends unless an auto-send policy is explicitly set for that campaign.
- **Classify first, then answer exactly what they asked.** [BUCHAN] Use the triage table.
- **Drop any warmth-as-performance after a positive reply.** Plain, warm, competent. Show you know your stuff. [BUCHAN]
- **Reply on their channel.** If they ask for a call, call. If they text, text. [HORMOZI]
- **Availability is throughput.** Offer lots of slots: 15-minute slots with 5-minute gaps. [HORMOZI]
- **Deliver the reply reward first,** the same day, before anything else. It was the promise that earned the reply. [HOUSE]
- Same copy rules as the doc: no dashes, no exclamation marks, no prices unless they ask "how much" (then the doc's "how much" template), one link max.

## 2. Triage table

| They said | Classification | Move |
|---|---|---|
| "Sure", "send it", the trigger word | Keen | Send the reward + 2 times + link (template K) |
| "When are you free?" | Keen, wants a call | 2 specific times + link (template K, no reward needed) |
| "Tell me more", "what is it?" | Curious | Short plain answer, the page/rundown, 2 times + link (template T) |
| "How much?" | Price question | Doc's "how much" template: the honest shape of the terms, then the walkthrough (template H) |
| "What's the catch?", "how do you make money?" | Trust question | Honest, short, terms stated plainly (template C) |
| "Not right now", with a date | Timing | Accept, set the date, keep it (template N1) |
| "Not right now", no date | Timing | Ask permission to check back in 3 months (template N2) |
| "Who are you / how did you get my email?" | Source question | Honest answer in one line, then offer to stop (template S) |
| "Not interested", polite | No | One-line apology, unsubscribe, never contact again (template X) |
| Rude, angry, "be more professional" | No | Unsubscribe. Do not reply. [BUCHAN] |
| Forwarded to a colleague | Referral | Thank the forwarder in one line, then write the colleague a fresh, short note referencing who sent them |
| Out of office | Auto | Not a reply. Harvest for the auto-responder seed list; resume after the return date |
| Late reply, months later | Keen | Treat as keen. Charming, honest emails get remembered. [BUCHAN] |

**Diagnose the mix, not the message.** A few negative replies are noise ("it's just words on the internet"). Only if replies are mostly negative, look at list, offer, copy, in that order. Never stop a campaign with an inbox full of positives because of one angry reply. [BUCHAN]

## 3. The booking cadence (after a keen reply, if they haven't booked) [ERIC, with credit to Oren Klaff]

1. **Reply 1, as fast as possible:** answer their question, send the reward, then two specific times plus the link. Some people are offended by a bare calendar link; others never book without one. Give both.
   > "I'm free Tuesday at 10:15 or Wednesday at 2:40 your time. Or if it's easier, grab any slot here: {calendar link}."
2. **+1 day, not booked:** the lost-times message. It reads like a busy person, not a "just following up".
   > "Hey {first_name}, I lost both of those times. Want to grab one on my calendar, or does Thursday at 11:20 or Friday at 9:45 work?"
3. **+2 days, not booked:** one line, written for this company only, on why the 15 minutes is worth it to them (from research: their site, their reviews, their signal). Then times or link.
4. **+2 days, not booked:** lost-times again, with fresh times.
5. **Then:** hand off to a caller (or Justin) by phone if a number exists. Then stop and set a 3-month recycle date.

Odd, specific times (10:15, 2:40) look like a real calendar, not a template. [BUCHAN odd-number principle]

## 4. Templates (adapt per doc, in the doc's voice)

**K. Keen**
> Hey {first_name}, here it is: {reward or page link}. {one line on what's inside}. If you want to walk through it, I'm free {time 1} or {time 2}, or grab any time here: {calendar link}.

**T. Tell me more**
> Hey {first_name}, sure. {two or three plain sentences: what it is, what happens, what it's built to do, the terms}. Here's the page: {page link}. Happy to walk you through it, {time 1} or {time 2} work on my end.

**H. How much**
> Hey {first_name}, fair question. {the honest shape of the terms, per the doc's offer block, no invented numbers}. The 15 minutes is where I sketch your numbers so you can judge it properly. {time 1} or {time 2}?

**C. What's the catch**
> Hey {first_name}, fair question. {the plain truth, per the doc's offer block}. Nothing hidden. Here's the page if you want to look first: {page link}.

**N1. Not now, with a date**
> Makes sense, {first_name}. I'll check back {their date}. {the reward, if not yet sent: "Here's the {thing} in the meantime, yours either way."}

**N2. Not now, no date**
> No problem at all, {first_name}. Mind if I check back in about three months? If not, just say so and I'll leave it with you.

**S. Who are you / how did you get my email**
> Fair question, {first_name}. I'm {sender}, {one plain line on what you do}. I found you through {honest source}. If you'd rather not hear from me, say the word and I'll take you off.

**X. Not interested (polite)**
> Understood, {first_name}. I won't contact you again. Best of luck with everything.

(Buchan's own version: "Apologies, {name}. I won't contact you again. Best of luck with everything. Have a great week." [BUCHAN])

## 5. After the call is booked

- Confirm in one line with the time in their time zone.
- The day before: a one-line reminder with one useful thing (their sketch, a question to think about). Cuts no-shows.
- No-show: one plain note with two new times. Then the caller.
- Log outcome to the CRM and the campaign's results note in the Obsidian vault (booked, showed, qualified, closed). The Analyst reads it.

## 6. Recycle

- No reply after the full sequence: recycle in 3 months with a new angle and, ideally, a new free thing. [HORMOZI 3 to 6 months] [BUCHAN 1 to 2 months]
- "Not now": the date they gave, or 3 months.
- Never recycle a no, an unsubscribe or a rude reply.
